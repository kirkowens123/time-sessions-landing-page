Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account?

No account required. Your data stays on your device—no sign-up, no login.

Is it free?

Tivvity offers a free version with core features. Pro features are available via subscription with pricing shown in-app.

Does it work offline?

Yes! All your data is stored locally on your device, so Tivvity works completely offline.

How do I export my data?

Export your time tracking data anytime in CSV or JSON format from the app settings.

How do I cancel Pro?

Manage your subscription anytime through your Apple ID settings. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal.
