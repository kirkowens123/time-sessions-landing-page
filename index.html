Everything You Need

Powerful features designed to help you track time effortlessly and achieve your goals

Visual Progress Tracking

Colorful bar charts and pie charts that show your progress at a glance. View Day, Week, Month, Year & All-time statistics.

Calendar Visualization

Visualize your time entries in a beautiful calendar interface with color-coded categories. See patterns and trends at a glance.

Quick Time Entry

Log time in seconds with our streamlined quick add modal. Select your category and activity, enter your time, and you're done.

Time, Frequency & Streak Goals

Set time-based, frequency, or streak goals for your activities. Stay motivated with progress tracking across Day, Week, Month, Year & All-time periods.

Data Export

Export your time tracking data anytime. Full control over your information with CSV or JSON export functionality.

Custom Categories

Create unlimited custom categories with emojis, then organize activities within them. Make tracking personal and fun.
