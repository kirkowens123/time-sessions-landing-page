Privacy Policy for Tivvity

Effective Date: February 2026

Tivvity (“we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy.

Information We Collect

Your time entries and personal data are stored locally on your device by default. We do not collect your name, email, or any personally identifying information.

We may collect limited, non-identifying analytics data to improve the app, including:

  • Device information (OS version, app version)
  • Feature usage patterns
  • Anonymous identifiers

We do not collect:

  • Names or email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Location data
  • Contacts
  • Photos, videos, or audio
  • Your actual time tracking data
  • Advertising or tracking identifiers

How We Use Information

Your data is stored locally on your device and is used solely for app functionality.

We do not use data for advertising or tracking.

Third-Party Services

Tivvity uses the following trusted third-party services:

  • RevenueCat — for subscription and purchase management
  • Apple In-App Purchases — for processing payments

These services may collect limited data as described in their respective privacy policies.

Analytics and Usage Data

We use PostHog, a privacy-focused analytics service, to understand how users interact with our app and improve the experience. This analytics collection is optional and can be disabled in the app settings.

PostHog collects only non-identifying usage data as described above. Your actual time tracking data never leaves your device. For more information about PostHog's data practices, visit: https://posthog.com/privacy

Data Retention

We retain user data only as long as necessary to provide app functionality.

Data Deletion

You can delete all app data by deleting the app from your device.

Auto-Renewable Subscriptions

Tivvity offers auto-renewable subscriptions. Payment is charged to your Apple ID account. Subscriptions automatically renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. No personal financial information is collected or stored by Tivvity.

Children's Privacy

Tivvity does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy occasionally. Updates will be posted on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact:
